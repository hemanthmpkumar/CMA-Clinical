\noindent\textbf{Manuscript title:} Geodesic Diagnostic Trajectories (GDT):
A Differential Geometry Framework for Adaptive Decision Support and Operational
Optimization in EHR Systems

\noindent\textbf{Journal:} IEEE Journal of Translational Engineering in Health
and Medicine (JTEHM)

\bigskip

\noindent In accordance with IEEE policy on conflicts of interest, the authors
of this manuscript make the following declarations:

\bigskip

\begin{enumerate}

\item \textbf{Financial interests.}
Neither author has any financial interest, direct or indirect, in any company,
product, or service related to the subject of this manuscript, including but not
limited to electronic health record systems, clinical decision support software,
information retrieval platforms, or healthcare IT vendors.

\item \textbf{Funding.}
This research received no specific grant from any funding agency in the public,
commercial, or not-for-profit sectors. No external funding was received for the
design, execution, analysis, or reporting of this study.

\item \textbf{Employment and affiliations.}
The authors are not employed by, and hold no paid consulting or advisory
relationships with, any organization whose financial interests could reasonably be
perceived as influencing the reported research.

\item \textbf{Data sources.}
All datasets used in this study (MIMIC-III, MIMIC-IV, eICU, HiRID) are publicly
available under their respective data use agreements. No proprietary clinical data
was accessed. The authors have no financial relationship with the custodians of
these datasets.

\item \textbf{Prior publications.}
This work builds on the Continuum Memory Architecture (CMA) described in earlier
preprints by the first author (CMA preprints, 2026). GDT is an extension
of that framework; the present manuscript reports new theoretical contributions
(Proposition~1), a new four-arm benchmark, and a new confidence-scaling mechanism
not present in the prior works. The overlap is fully disclosed in Sections~I
and~II of the manuscript and in the Previously Published Statement filed separately.

\item \textbf{Personal relationships.}
Neither author has a personal relationship with any editor or reviewer of this
journal that could be perceived as influencing the peer review process.

\item \textbf{Non-financial interests.}
The authors have no non-financial competing interests (ideological, personal,
academic, or otherwise) that could have influenced this work.

\end{enumerate}
