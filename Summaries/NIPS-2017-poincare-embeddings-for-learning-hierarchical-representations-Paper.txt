## Extracted Abstract / Overview
Representation learning has become an invaluable approach for learning from sym- bolic data such as text and graphs. However, state-of-the-art embedding methods typically do not account for latent hierarchical structures which are characteristic for many complex symbolic datasets. In this work, we introduce a new approach for learning hierarchical representations of symbolic data by embedding them into hyperbolic space – or more precisely into an n-dimensional Poincaré ball. Due to the underlying hyperbolic geometry, this allows us to learn parsimonious repre- sentations of symbolic data by simultaneously capturing hierarchy and similarity. We present an efﬁcient algorithm to learn the embeddings based on Riemannian optimization and show experimentally that Poincaré embeddings can outperform Euclidean embeddings signiﬁcantly on data with latent hierarchies, both in terms of representation capacity and in terms of generalization ability.
