## Extracted Abstract / Overview
Background: Artificial Intelligence as a Medical Device (AIaMD) introduces regulatory, methodological, ethical, and clinical challenges that are not fully addressed by traditional device trial frameworks. Given rapidly evolving and jurisdiction-specific guidance, a consolidated mapping of trial design expectations and regulatory requirements is needed.
