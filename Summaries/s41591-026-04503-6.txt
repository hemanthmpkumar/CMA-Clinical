Clinical safety. Out of 1,000 LLM outputs associated with red alerts (prompted by the LLM’s interpretation of the documented informa- tion being incorrect/harmful), which were reviewed by the expert panel, 494 (49.4%) were rated as definitely safe and appropriate, and 424 (42.4%) as mostly safe and appropriate. In addition, 40 (4.0%) were considered neutral (neither unsafe nor inappropriate), while 31 (3.1%) were judged somewhat unsafe or inappropriate, and 11 (1.1%) as unsafe and inappropriate. Clinicians fully adhered to the LLM’s advice in 195 encounters (19.5%), partially adhered in 573 encounters (57.3%) and did not adhere in 232 encounters (23.2%).
